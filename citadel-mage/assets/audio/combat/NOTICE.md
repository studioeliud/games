# Combat audio

The combat cues in this directory are mono PCM mixes trimmed, resampled, and
level-matched for this game. Some mixes layer the project's original cues
from the parent audio directory.

- **Kenney · RPG Audio (CC0)**. `knifeSlice.ogg`, `knifeSlice2.ogg`,
  `creak2.ogg`, `chop.ogg`, and `metalClick.ogg`. Used for release and hit
  transients. [Pack and license](https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects)
- **Kenney · Impact Sounds and Sci-fi Sounds (CC0)**. Mining and plate
  impacts, and `explosionCrunch_000.ogg`. Used in the projectile and frost
  impacts and cannon report. [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) ·
  [Sci-fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) ·
  [Distribution filenames](https://github.com/manuel-palacio/brickstorm/blob/main/CREDITS.md)
- **JaggedStone · Magic Spell SFX (CC0)**. `magical_1.ogg` through
  `magical_4.ogg`. Used in the six elemental casts and bursts.
  [Pack and license](https://opengameart.org/node/29772)
